Rhythm Light · three forms

Type below. All three read the same keystrokes.

Each is loud enough to satisfy X-1 and each survives reduced motion, so the choice is about what the light says while it works. Pick one and I'll take it through all fourteen frames.

A recommended
Concentric
A ring leaves the dot on every key. Reads as a heartbeat — presence, not progress. Cheapest to ship and the easiest to place beside a field.
B

Seven bars, one per recent key, height from the gap before it. The only form that shows the rhythm itself rather than the fact of it — and the only one that could be mistaken for a level meter.

The ring closes as keys land. Legible at a glance but it implies a target length, which the passphrase does not have — and Phantom Keys makes that reading actively wrong.

Three bands

Green, amber, red — kept off the accent entirely.

Amber is instantly legible and it does carry a warning reading, so nothing else amplifies it: no filled panel, no icon, no exclamation. It gets a line and a dot, and the copy does the de-escalating.

Pass ≥ 0.62
In you go. The common case, and it should feel unremarkable — green appears for about a second and never announces itself.
Amber 0.45 – 0.62
Probably you, on a different day. Retype once, then a Backup Code. Named “amber” in the UI, still “grey” in the code and the docs.
Fail < 0.45
Not your rhythm. Refused, and an email is sent. Band, score and reason — nothing further, ever.

CypherKey Recovery Kit
Issued 12 March 2026
mreyes · m.reyes@hey.com

This sheet is the only way back into your password vault.

CypherKey is a password manager. It normally unlocks with a passphrase you type in a particular rhythm, and neither the passphrase nor the rhythm leaves your computer — which means nobody at CypherKey can let you back in. This code can.

Using it costs you your rhythm profile. Your vault and its contents survive. You will choose a new passphrase and teach it your typing again, which takes about three minutes. Keep this sheet somewhere a burglar would not look and a fire would not reach.

3LBCG 6YZEA 5UK
Enter this at the “Use your Recovery Kit” link on the unlock screen. It works once, then this sheet is spent and you will be given a new one. Entering it discards your rhythm profile.
